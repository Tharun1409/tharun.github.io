About

Who I am and what I am good at.

I am a full stack engineer on the Microsoft stack, with C#, ASP.NET MVC, .NET Core, WCF and SQL Server at the centre of my work. Most of my career has gone into systems where a wrong number has a real cost: securities settlement, cash movement and depot balances at Euroclear Bank, and analytics reporting at Cigna.

I have more than 12 years of functional knowledge in settlements, corporate actions, cash transfers, depot management and collateral management. On the technical side I am strongest at integrating .NET with IBM MQ, building reusable Web API components, tuning SQL, and shipping on Azure with CI/CD pipelines.

I also lead: pair programming, mentoring, architecture reviews and production support are part of how I work.
